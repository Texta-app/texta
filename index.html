FAQ

Common questions.

What is a text segment?

A standard text message holds up to 160 characters. That's one segment — one cent. If your message is longer, it splits into multiple segments at one cent each. Most short messages are a single segment, and the app shows the segment count as you type so there are no surprises.

What about emojis and special characters?

Certain characters — curly quotes, em dashes, some emojis — trigger Unicode encoding that reduces segment size to 70 characters. The app warns you the moment this happens and shows exactly which character is causing it. For best results, stick to plain text.

Do I pay to receive replies?

The first 500 inbound messages per month are on us. After that, replies cost the same as an outgoing text — one cent per segment. Most accounts never come close to 500. Your dashboard always shows your current inbound count so you know exactly where you stand.

How do replies work?

Every inbound reply shows up in your Replies tab, matched to the contact name if we recognize the number. You get an email notification when someone responds so you never miss it. If a contact replies STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT, END, or CANCEL, they're automatically removed from future sends — no manual work.

What is the link shortener and do I need it?

If you include a URL in your message, Texta automatically shortens it to a go.texta.me link before sending. It just happens. This saves characters (which saves money) and lets you see exactly how many people clicked, right in your send history. Long URLs can eat a big chunk of your 160-character segment, so the shortener is a win on both cost and analytics.

What happens when my credits run low?

Set up auto-recharge so your balance tops up automatically when it drops below a threshold you choose. Or add credits manually any time. Either way you stay in control and your campaigns never get cut off mid-send.

Can I send images?

Yes. Image messages (MMS) cost 2.5 cents each, regardless of file size. Attach photos, promotional graphics, or GIFs directly in the compose window. That's still cheaper than the major competitors charge for plain text.

Can I import my existing contacts?

Yes. Import via CSV, add them manually, use the Zapier integration to pull them from your existing tools, or use a Texta sign-up form to let people opt in directly. All contacts live in one place and you can group them however makes sense.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees, no awkward phone calls. Cancel from your billing settings in under a minute. Your unused credits never expire — if you cancel and come back later, they're still waiting for you.
