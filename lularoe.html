FAQ

Questions retailers ask.

Does the BLESS import really work automatically? +

Yes. Every BLESS customer export has the same column format, so Texta recognizes it automatically. You do not have to tell us which column is the phone number or match anything up. Drop the file in, we handle the rest. First name, last name, phone number, and birthday all come in clean.

What if I have duplicate customers in BLESS? +

Texta checks for duplicate phone numbers during import and skips any duplicates automatically. You will see a summary after every import showing how many contacts were added and how many were skipped as duplicates.

Is it legal to text my LuLaRoe customers? +

If your customers gave you their phone number and you have an existing business relationship with them, you are generally on solid ground for informational and promotional messages. That said, you are responsible for ensuring you have appropriate consent. Texta automatically includes opt-out language ("Reply STOP to unsubscribe") on every message by default, which is required by law. When someone replies STOP, they are removed from your list automatically and permanently. We strongly recommend consulting with a legal professional if you have any questions about compliance.

Can I update my contacts when I get new customers in BLESS? +

Yes. You can re-import a fresh BLESS export at any time. Texta adds new contacts and skips anyone already in your list, so there is no risk of duplication. Your opted-out customers are never re-activated by an import — that protection is permanent.

What does 10DLC mean and do I have to deal with it? +

10DLC is a registration system the major carriers use to identify legitimate businesses sending text messages. Without it, your messages may be filtered as spam. Texta walks you through the registration process as part of setup — it is a one-time thing, takes about 10 minutes of your time, and typically gets approved within 1 to 2 weeks. After that you are set for good.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Your unused message credits never expire — if you cancel and come back later, your credits are still waiting for you.
