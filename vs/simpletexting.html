Switching is easy

Three steps and you're done.

Most accounts are up and running in under an hour. Your existing contacts come with you.

1
Export your contacts from SimpleTexting
In your SimpleTexting dashboard, go to Contacts and export as a CSV. It takes about 30 seconds. Keep the file — you'll need it in step 2.
2
Import into Texta in one click
Create your Texta account, go to Contacts, and upload the CSV. Texta recognizes the SimpleTexting export format automatically — no column-matching required.
3
Complete 10DLC and send
Texta walks you through 10DLC registration step by step. Once approved (typically a few days), send your first campaign. Let your list know you moved — and keep going.
